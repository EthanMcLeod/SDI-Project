Title: MyCiTi Bus Transit Network

Summary: Routes and operational stops for the MyCiTi Bus Rapid Transit (BRT) system in Cape Town.

Keywords: transit, bus, routes, stops, transport, myciti, coct_transport

Abstract: This dataset contains official operational transport features for the City of Cape Town's MyCiTi bus service. It includes spatial line vectors mapping out transit route coverages along with point layers referencing municipal bus stop stations.
